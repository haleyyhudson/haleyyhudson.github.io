My Teaching Philosophy

I am committed to fostering an inclusive and engaging learning environment that emphasizes critical thinking, hands-on experience, and skills development. I believe students should be encouraged to push-themselves in a safe learning environment with opportunities to practice skills and concepts, get feedback, and improve. I approach students in a humble compassionate way that acknowledges the humanity of each of them.
