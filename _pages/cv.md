Research and Work Experience
======
* Graduate Research Assistant — Department of Integrative Biology, Oregon State University, September 2022-Present  
  * Advisor: Dr. Francis Chan  
  * Investigate climate change stressors to marine resources, study hypoxia impacts on Dungeness crab, and map spatial/temporal variability of hypoxia and ocean acidification.  

* Graduate Teaching Assistant — OSU Biology Courses (BI 221: Cells, BI 222: Organisms, BI 223: Ecosystems), September 2022-Present, 17 hrs/week  
  * Supervisor: Lori Kayes and Carmen Harjoe  
  * Conduct lab instruction, deliver lectures, grade, hold office hours, write syllabi, and facilitate course-based undergraduate research experiences.  

* Biological Science Assistant — Oregon Department of Fish and Wildlife, April 2022-June 2022, 40 hrs/week  
  * Crew Lead: Richard Hays  
  * Field sampling, fish identification, habitat surveys, GPS mapping, and data collection for population estimates.  

* Scientific Aide — California Department of Water Resources, Aquatic Ecology Section, June 2020-June 2021, 40 hrs/week  
  * Supervisors: Brian Schreier, Nicole Kwan, Melinda Baerwald  
  * Fish sampling, water quality measurement, zooplankton collection, data analysis in R, manuscript co-authorship, experimental design, and technical report writing.  

* Fisheries and Conservation Technician — Bodega Marine Laboratory, UC Davis, June–Sept 2019 & 2018, 40 hrs/week  
  * Advisor: Dr. Steven Morgan  
  * Fish tagging and measurement, krill abundance analysis, wet lab maintenance, marine protected area studies, and community outreach.  

* Senior Thesis — Rockfishes in Marine Protected Areas, Bodega Marine Laboratory, UC Davis, Sept 2019-June 2020  
  * Advisor: Dr. Steven Morgan  
  * ArcGIS mapping, R analysis, figure generation, statistical tests on rockfish responses to marine protected areas.  

* Independent Research — Bivalve Salinity Tolerance, Tropical Ecology and Conservation, UC Education Abroad Program, Costa Rica, March–June 2019  
  * Advisor: Dr. Frank Joyce  
  * Experimental design, bivalve collection, filtration rate measurements, and community engagement.  

* Environmental Genomics Researcher — Department of Environmental Toxicology, UC Davis, Oct 2017-June 2020  
  * Advisor: Dr. Andrew Whitehead  
  * NGS library prep, RNA sequencing analysis, aquatic facility work, tissue collection, and lab meetings.  

* Neurogenomics Researcher — Department of Neurobiology, Physiology and Behavior, UC Davis, Sept 2016-June 2017  
  * Advisor: Dr. Rebecca Calisi  
  * PCR, qPCR, tissue sectioning, animal care, and gene expression studies.
